# InterTune

InterTune is a music player for Android. It plays YouTube Music, and it plays music files you already keep on your phone or SD card. It is a fork of [OuterTune](https://github.com/OuterTune/OuterTune), which means it started life as a copy of that app and then went its own way. Upstream OuterTune stopped supporting YouTube Music in February 2026 and has not been touched at all since April 2026, so InterTune carries its own fixes, the main one being that YouTube Music songs play all the way through again instead of cutting out after about half a minute. It is one person's project, kept public because other people find it useful. If something is broken, say so on [InterTune's issues](https://github.com/ItzSkyeYT/InterTune/issues) rather than upstream's, because upstream is not reading theirs.

# InterTune 0.10.9.5

A small update on top of 0.10.9, so the Discord server can reach you before 0.11, with a few fixes from 0.11 that should not wait for it.

- **News from InterTune.** Announcements about the app, with their own switch: you are asked once after the update, and it is already on if you said yes to questions. They open full screen like a question, with pictures and up to three buttons. The first is about the new Discord server: https://discord.gg/68jmqhMjXk
- **Links you can tap** in questions and announcements, which can also have paragraphs, lists and bold text.
- **Discord in About**, next to GitHub.
- **A count of who opened a question or an announcement.** Opening one is counted once, anonymously: its id, its title and the app version, nothing about you or your device. Answers were already sent the same way. With questions and news both off, nothing is fetched and nothing is sent.

## Fixes

- **Lyrics** were often missing for songs started from search, even obvious ones, and every song was looked up twice. Songs named in Chinese or Japanese now find their lyrics on KuGou whichever script they are written in.
- **The player's buttons** no longer shrink when lyrics are on.
- **A long title** fades out before the buttons beside it, instead of stopping mid-letter against them as if it ran on underneath.

## New, off by default

- **Queue button** (Settings > Look and feel, under Behavior, with the swipe gestures): opens the queue from a button beside the title instead of pulling it up from the bottom of the player. Like and the menu stay where they are.

Nothing else changed.

# InterTune 0.11: Quick picks that learn how you listen

Banner: 0.11 is here: Quick picks that learn how you listen

0.11 adds **Best recommendations**, a Quick picks source built on your phone from how you actually listen: what you finish, skip, search for, like and come back to. Nothing about it leaves your phone.

- Each card says why it is there, and **Why these?** on the heading shows the songs the row was built around.
- It learns from what you play and what you pass over, a little at a time.
- Long-press a card for Not this song, Less of this artist or Never this artist.
- Dials for Adventurousness and Familiarity, and chips for Discover, Favourites, Focus, Chill and Party.

It is experimental and finds its feet over the first week. **Try both** mixes it with your old row, card for card, so you can see which wins.

If you updated, you are still on the source you had. Switch under Settings > Library and content > Quick picks source.

Also new: **What's playing?** names the song around you (tap the waveform in the search bar), and a home screen widget.

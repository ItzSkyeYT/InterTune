# InterTune 0.11: Quick picks that learn how you listen

Banner: 0.11 is here: Quick picks that learn how you listen

0.11 adds **Best recommendations**, a Quick picks source built on your phone from how you actually listen: what you finish, skip, search for, like and come back to. Nothing about it leaves your phone unless you say yes to similar songs from Last.fm, which is then sent the title and artist of what you play.

- Each card says why it is there, and a tap on the Quick picks heading opens **Why these?**, the songs the row was built around.
- It learns from what you play and what you pass over, a little at a time.
- Long-press a card for Not this song, Less of this artist or Never this artist.
- Dials for Adventurousness and Familiarity, and chips for Discover, Favourites, Focus, Chill and Party.

Songs you have never heard was the top answer to the question about Quick picks, so there is now a **Discover something new** row, and the Discover chip gives them half of Quick picks. The other answers got a setting: **Quick picks leans toward**, under Settings > Recommendations, gives most of Best recommendations' cards to songs you have never heard, artists you keep coming back to, things you loved and forgot about, or what you are playing now.

It is experimental and finds its feet over the first week. **Try both** mixes it card for card with YouTube Music's picks, or your library's when YouTube has none, so you can see which wins.

If you updated, you are still on the source you had. Switch under Settings > Library and content > Quick picks source.

Also new: **What's playing?** names the song around you, using Shazam (tap the waveform in the search bar), a home screen widget, and spatial audio, with head tracking on supported headphones.

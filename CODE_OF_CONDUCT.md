# Code of conduct

InterTune is made in people's free time and used all over the world. This page says how we treat each other wherever the project lives: here on GitHub, on the Discord server, on Weblate, and in anything sent through intertune.app.

## Be kind

- Be patient with people who are new, who write in their second language, or who report a bug you cannot reproduce.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Say what you think, then listen.
- Keep to the topic, and remember that everyone here gives their time for free.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, threats or slurs, in public or in private messages.
- Hate or mockery aimed at who someone is: where they come from, their skin colour, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age, or anything else about them.
- Sharing someone's private details without their permission.
- Sexual content, spam and scams.
- Carrying on after someone has asked you to stop.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks these rules, send a direct message to a moderator on Discord, or write to intertune.dev@gmail.com. Reports are read only by the maintainer and the moderators, and stay private. Nobody gets in trouble for a report made in good faith.

## What happens next

The moderators decide what fits what happened: a quiet word, a warning, a removed message, a timeout, or a ban from the project's spaces. These rules apply to the maintainer and the moderators too.

This code is our own, inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

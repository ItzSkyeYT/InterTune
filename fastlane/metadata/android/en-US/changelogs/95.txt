Version 0.12

- Cast to Chromecast, FCast or a DLNA TV.
- Radios learn from your skips and likes.
- Living blur: the player's background moves with the music.
- An equalizer, with your headphones' own curve.
- Share lyrics as text, a picture or a video, or read them in Latin letters.
- The sleep timer can start itself every day.
- Hide explicit songs and music videos.
- "What's playing?" names sped-up and slowed songs.

Full changelog: https://github.com/ItzSkyeYT/InterTune/releases/tag/v0.12

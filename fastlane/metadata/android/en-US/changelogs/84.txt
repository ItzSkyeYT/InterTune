Version 0.10.7

=== Changes and improvements ===
• Both opt-ins, updates and questions, are now asked once after an update or after restoring a backup, so they are no longer invisible to anyone who skipped setup
• Refreshing Home reads past the first response and genuinely finds new songs, roughly four times as much to draw from
• A + button on the library screen for creating a playlist
• An empty playlist now turns into a search for its first song instead of being a dead end
• Tapping a question notification opens the question rather than just the app

Full changelog: https://github.com/ItzSkyeYT/InterTune/releases/tag/v0.10.7

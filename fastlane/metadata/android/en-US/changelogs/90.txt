Version 0.11

Quick picks that learn how you listen, and InterTune can name the song playing around you. Most of what is new stays off until you turn it on.

=== Recommendations ===
• Best recommendations: a Quick picks row built from what you finish, search for, like and come back to. New installs start on it, updates keep their source
• Discover something new: a second row of songs you have never played
• Why these?, dials for adventurousness and familiarity, and Not this song, Less of this artist or Never this artist on a long press
• Similar songs from YouTube and, if you say yes, Last.fm

=== Song recognition ===
• Tap the waveform in the search bar: Listen once, or Keep listening in the background and build a playlist from what it hears

=== Player and sound ===
• Spatial audio for any headphones, with head tracking where they support it
• Fade between tracks, a new seekbar, a sleep timer button, and Connected buttons as an option
• The queue can open from a button instead of the sheet

=== Home screen ===
• A widget with what is playing and a list, set up per widget

=== Fixes ===
• Lyrics found for songs played from search, and looked up once
• Saved queues, queue search, playlist search and synced playlist drags act on the right songs
• Many more, see the full changelog

Full changelog: https://github.com/ItzSkyeYT/InterTune/releases/tag/v0.11

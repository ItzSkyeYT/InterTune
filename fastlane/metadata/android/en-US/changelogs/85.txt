Version 0.10.8

=== New ===
• Automatic backups: pick a folder, pick every day or every week, and pick how many to keep on a slider. Your library, playlists, settings and listening history. Off until you turn it on
• Save the queue as a playlist, from a button on the queue sheet, in the order shown
• Export every playlist as an M3U file, in one tap, into a folder you choose

=== Fixes ===
• Music no longer sticks on "Wait to reconnect" until you force close the app. One failed request on a healthy connection could hold a song for good
• "Let other apps play at the same time" now takes effect when you turn it on, instead of doing nothing until the app's process was killed
• Songs no longer arrive with a play count or an upload date where the artist should be
• A back button on the mini player, which only had play and next
• Skipping is lighter: every song change used to ask the settings file four questions, three of them on the thread that draws the screen

=== Quick picks ===
• The row no longer blanks on every refresh, and Home no longer shows two rows both called Quick picks
• On the YouTube Music source the row is YouTube's own Quick picks shelf, so a feed carrying a shelf of hour-long mixes no longer puts those under the heading
• A card you play stays where it is, playing, until you refresh
• Pull to refresh brings new songs, not the same ones in a new order
• Two long-standing defects in the library query were fixed, and live, slowed and remastered versions of one song are no longer shown side by side
• The row arrives about four seconds sooner, because it no longer waits behind the requests that fill the rows further down the screen

=== Interface ===
• Predictive back and reveal transitions
• An Offline tab: the songs that will play with no connection
• Remove a song from its playlist without leaving the player
• Resume playback when the app opens, as a setting
• A Highest available audio tier, and the settings now say what is actually playing
• The app says so when the song cache is turned off

=== Notes ===
• Installs from F-Droid update through F-Droid; the in-app updater steps aside for them
• The app starts logging what you play so that the recommendation work coming next arrives to a history rather than to nothing. It stays on this device. The Quick picks improvements above are the only things reading it, and Settings > Privacy and history > Pause listen history turns it off

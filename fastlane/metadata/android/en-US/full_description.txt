<p><i>InterTune</i> is a fork of <a href="https://github.com/OuterTune/OuterTune" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">OuterTune</a>, which is itself a fork of <a href="https://github.com/z-huang/InnerTune" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">InnerTune</a>. It is both a local media player and a YouTube Music client.</p>

<p><br><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>YouTube Music client with song downloading for offline playback, background playback, no ads, and account synchronization</li>
<li>Local audio file playback (MP3, OGG, FLAC, and more)</li>
<li>A custom tag extractor rather than MediaStore's metadata extractor, so tags delimited with a backslash show up properly</li>
<li>Play local files and YouTube Music songs in the same queue</li>
<li>An offline tab listing the songs already cached on the device, so you can see what will play with no connection</li>
<li>Integrated library screen design</li>
<li>Multiple queues</li>
<li>Synchronized lyrics, including word by word and karaoke lyrics (LRC, TTML)</li>
<li>Audio normalization, tempo and pitch adjustment, and other audio effects</li>
<li>Choose whether Quick picks comes from YouTube Music or is built from what you have already played</li>
<li>Android Auto support</li>
</ul>

<p><br><b>What it connects to:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>YouTube Music, for everything you search, play, download or sync. Signing in is optional.</li>
<li>LRCLIB and KuGou, to find lyrics for the song that is playing. Each can be turned off.</li>
<li>Shazam, only while you have it listening. A sound fingerprint is sent, with the time and your time zone, never the recording. The cover of a song it finds is loaded from the address Shazam gives.</li>
<li>Last.fm, only if you turn it on. To find similar songs it is sent the title and artist of songs you play, and once of the songs you liked or played in the last month, with no account. If you connect a Last.fm account, the songs you play are also recorded on it, with album, length and the time they started. You sign in on Last.fm's own page.</li>
<li>GitHub, only if you turn on the update check or tap Check for update yourself. The app can then download a new release and hand it to Android's installer, which always asks you first. A copy installed from F-Droid is sent to F-Droid for its update instead.</li>
<li>Polls and announcements, only if you turn them on. They are read from a GitHub gist, and a picture in one is loaded from the address it gives. Opening or answering one is counted on Umami Cloud, with no account and no device identifier.</li>
<li>The usage count, only if you turn it on. Once a day at most it tells Umami Cloud the app is in use: a random name that changes every month, whether it was installed from F-Droid, and the app and Android versions.</li>
</ul>

<p><br><b>Notice:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Android 8 (Oreo) and higher is supported. The app may work on Android 7.x, but that is not officially supported.</li>
<li>InterTune is in a stable beta phase. It can certainly be used as your main music player, but expect the occasional bug, incomplete feature, or unexpected behaviour. Please report anything you find.</li>

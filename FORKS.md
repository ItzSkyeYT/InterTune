# Forks of InterTune

InterTune is GPL-3.0: you may fork it, change it and share it. These rules are not optional, and they apply before you share a single build.

1. **Rename it.** Your fork gets its own name and its own icon everywhere: the app, the repository, the README and any store listing. "InterTune" and its logo stay with this app.
2. **Change the app ID.** Replace `dev.skye.intertune` with your own `applicationId` in your first commit. A build that installs as InterTune gets mistaken for it and breaks people's updates.
3. **Cut InterTune's services.** The update check, news and questions, install counts, Report a problem, the share page, the Discord, the translation project and the donors list belong to InterTune. Point them at your own or remove them. A build not signed with InterTune's key switches them off by itself.
4. **Your users are yours.** Keep Issues turned on in your repository, so your users can report problems to you. Bug reports, questions and support for your fork go to you, never to InterTune.
5. **Credit and licence.** Say plainly that your fork is based on InterTune and OuterTune, keep GPL-3.0 and every copyright notice, and publish the full source of every build you share.

A fork that keeps InterTune's name, icon, app ID or services after being told about these rules will be reported to GitHub.
